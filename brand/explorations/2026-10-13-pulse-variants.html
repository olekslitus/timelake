Pulse: B2 refined, plus the full set

New at the top (F): twelve B2 versions that fill the circle and give the bottom line a clear first rise. With three heavy lines that fill the circle, perfectly even spacing and a tall bottom rise can't both fit, so these blend part of the way from parallel (even gaps) toward identical copies (full rise on every line). "Blend" is how parallel it stays: 0.25 favors the bottom rise, 0.5 favors even gaps. C is the earlier B2 round. In a parallel design, each lower line has to rise less than the one above it; that is why B2's bottom line became a tiny bump. Taller spikes, lower-angle slopes, drawing the full spike on the bottom line (bottom anchored), and blending parallel with copies all keep the bottom rise strong. Below that, the original two families.

Two families. A stacks identical copies of the pulse, like v0, so the gaps narrow on the slopes. B uses truly parallel lines, like v0.1, so the gap is the same everywhere and the outer lines become nested chevrons. Each card shows paper and ink at 120, 48, 32 and 16 px, plus the lockup. Tell me the IDs you like, or mix them ("B4 but with A2's stroke").
